## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-sharp, high-trust editorial aesthetic for wholesale telecommunications distribution. Designed specifically for Korean telecommunications retail business operators, the interface bridges institutional reliability with modern Swiss-grotesk minimalism. It rejects consumer-facing SaaS trends—deliberately eliminating decorative gradients, drop shadows, playful iconography, and illustrative fluff in favor of architectural structure, dramatic scale contrasts, and stark typographic authority.

The brand persona is confident, taciturn, and surgically precise. It evokes the feeling of an authoritative tier-1 telecom infrastructure backbone:
- **Tone:** Executive, uncompromised, hyper-organized, industrial-grade reliability.
- **Visual Style:** Editorial Minimalism rooted in Neo-Grotesk structure, drawing inspiration from high-end architectural monographs and the Poetic design language.
- **Key Motif:** The solid green architectural anchor block (an 18px square) positioned strictly at the top-right of primary cards and locked beside the master wordmark, functioning as a silent hallmark of enterprise authentication.

## Colors

The color system operates on extreme value contrasts paired with deliberate chromatic restraint. It alternates between luminous, warm-white expanses (`#FFFFFA`) and deep, absorbing monolithic black blocks (`#0B0D12`).

### Color Roles & Mechanics
- **Base Canvas (`#FFFFFA`):** An ivory warm-white that eliminates sterile clinical glare while providing a refined paper-like editorial baseline.
- **Monolith Black (`#0B0D12`):** A high-density deep black containing slight cool-blue undertones, used for section inversions, high-priority bento containers, and dominant CTAs.
- **Accent Forest Green (`#1E6F50`):** The singular chromatic driver. Reserved strictly for operational status confirmation, active indicators, the 18px geometric seal, and primary brand accents.
- **Tinted Celadon Green (`#DCEBE3`):** Applied exclusively for two-tone unfilled headline treatments, subtle badges, and inactive structural pairings against deep black or off-white.
- **Structural Hairlines (`#E5E5DF` / `#1E222B`):** 1px mechanical dividers providing pure grid definitions without simulated depth.

Gradients and decorative chromatic washes are strictly prohibited. Two-tone typography achieves emphasis by breaking monochrome statements with inline `#DCEBE3` or `#1E6F50` spans.

## Typography

The typographic system is built upon modern Grotesk forms characterized by solid horizontal stress, neutral terminals, and tight tracking. For production deployments with bilingual Latin-Hangul typography, Hanken Grotesk maps natively to Pretendard/Pretendard Variable for Hangul characters.

### Editorial Rules
- **Break Mechanics:** All Korean headline and body text must enforce `word-break: keep-all;` and `overflow-wrap: break-word;` to eliminate unnatural line splits in B2B terms (e.g., 동시개통, 결합할인, 회선수수료).
- **Two-Tone Headlines:** Expressive typographic rhythm is achieved by setting the primary phrase in solid `#0B0D12` (or `#FFFFFA` on dark surfaces) and highlighting qualifying statements in `#DCEBE3` or `#1E6F50`.
- **Labels & System Meta:** Monospaced data tags, commission brackets, carrier IDs, and technical specifications must be displayed in `Space Mono` uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`).

## Layout & Spacing

Layout in this design system creates confidence through negative space. It relies on a rigorous 12-column grid paired with monumental structural gaps.

### Spacing Principles
- **Monumental Section Breaks:** Major thematic content shifts on desktop maintain a strict `10rem` (160px) vertical spacing rhythm (`section-gap`). On mobile viewpoints, this contracts cleanly to `5rem` (80px).
- **Bento & Data Grid:** Structured around a 12-column desktop grid with a `2rem` (32px) gutter. Cards snap strictly to 3, 4, 6, or 12 column spans.
- **Section Rhythm:**
  - Desktop (1280px+): Max width 1440px centered; lateral margin `5rem`.
  - Tablet (768px - 1279px): 8-column layout; gutter `1.5rem`; lateral margin `3rem`.
  - Mobile (0px - 767px): 4-column layout; gutter `1rem`; lateral margin `1.5rem`.
- **Card Breathing Room:** Card inner paddings scale uniformly between `2rem` (tablet/desktop) and `1.25rem` (mobile), providing consistent density.

## Elevation & Depth

This system is completely planar. Drop shadows, Gaussian blurs, ambient ambient-occlusion tints, and layered z-index elevations are entirely absent.

### Techniques for Depth & Separation
- **High-Contrast Surface Juxtaposition:** Depth is established by placing solid `#0B0D12` tectonic plates atop the warm `#FFFFFA` field.
- **1px Hairline Borders:** Structural partitions are defined solely by crisp, non-blurred 1px borders using `#E5E5DF` on light surfaces and `#1E222B` on dark containers.
- **Overlapping Framing:** Information hierarchy is established by nesting smaller high-contrast modules within larger monoliths, creating an architectural blueprint feel rather than a physical floating illusion.
- **Hover & Active States:** Interactive surfaces never elevate along the Z-axis. Instead, they react via 1px border value shifts (transitioning from `#E5E5DF` to `#0B0D12` or `#1E6F50`) or crisp color inversions.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a deliberate dichotomy between structural containers and interactive controls:

### Structural Geometry (Cards & Panels)
- Large visual containers, section blocks, bento grids, and data frames utilize sharp to minimally softened geometry (`8px` to `12px` maximum corner radius) to preserve an architectural, stable presence.
- Inner elements nest concentric radii without sloppy overlaps.

### Interactive Geometry (Pills)
- All interactive controls—including action buttons, search pills, filter chips, and status tags—use the full `pill` form factor (`roundedness: 3`, border-radius 9999px). This provides an immediate, ergonomic distinction between the structural layout grid and clickable surfaces.

### The Enterprise Anchor Motif
- An unrounded, razor-sharp `18px × 18px` square filled with `#1E6F50` sits pinned precisely to the top-right corner of all primary `#0B0D12` cards (inset `24px` from borders) and directly adjacent to the primary wordmark. It stands completely unrounded to contrast with the pill-shaped buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Pill):** Background `#0B0D12`, text `#FFFFFA`, zero border, border-radius `9999px`. High-padding ergonomics (`14px 28px`), typography `Hanken Grotesk 14px bold`. Hover state transitions background to `#1E6F50`.
- **Secondary Action (Pill):** Background `#F0F0EB`, text `#0B0D12`, border `1px solid transparent`, border-radius `9999px`. Hover state applies `#E5E5DF` background and hairline `#0B0D12` outline.
- **Inverted Dark Mode Pill:** For inside deep-black modules. Background `#FFFFFA`, text `#0B0D12`, hovering to `#DCEBE3`.

### Interactive Chips & Status Badges
- **Commission & Rate Badges:** Pill shape, height `24px`, padding `0 12px`. Set in `Space Mono 11px bold`. 
- **Active Activation Indicator:** Outlined pill using `#1E6F50` hairline, containing a solid 6px `#1E6F50` circular pulse dot followed by uppercase status text.

### Monolithic Cards
- **Deep Black Hero Card:** Background `#0B0D12`, text `#FFFFFA`, border `1px solid #1E222B`, radius `12px`. Always features the mandatory `18px × 18px` solid `#1E6F50` square in the top-right corner with `24px` margin-top and margin-right offset.
- **Warm White Data Card:** Background `#FFFFFA`, border `1px solid #E5E5DF`, radius `12px`. Internal sections delineated by horizontal `#E5E5DF` hairline dividers.

### Form Inputs
- **Wholesale Portal Field:** Flat, clean baseline fields. Background `#FFFFFF`, height `52px`, padding `0 18px`, border `1px solid #E5E5DF`, border-radius `8px`. Active focus shifts border to `1px solid #0B0D12` with zero outer glow.
- **Helper & Numeric Labels:** Placed above inputs in `Space Mono 11px uppercase`, color `#6E7178`.

### Wholesale Activation Tables & Data Lists
- Stripped of vertical column grid lines.
- Horizontal row items separated strictly by `1px solid #E5E5DF` lines.
- Row padding: `20px` top and bottom.
- Carrier tags (SK, KT, LG U+) display in compact uppercase mono badges with high contrast.
